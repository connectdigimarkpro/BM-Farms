About BM Farms

Leading the Region in Automated Contract Poultry Management

Established in 2018 in Village Birampur, Hoshiarpur, Punjab

BM Farms represents a paradigm shift in modern broiler production, specializing in professional **Contract Broiler Poultry Farming**. We do not operate a retail shop; instead, we serve as a trusted, high-volume production partner for major integrated poultry enterprises.

Equipped with Belgium-imported **Roxell automated infrastructure**, our facility achieves industry-leading efficiency in feed conversion, bird comfort, and hygiene control. Every shed operates with climate-controlled automated ventilation, honeycomb cooling pads, and micro-sprinkler drinking arrays to prioritize animal welfare and operational safety.

Our Infrastructure

High-Tech Facilities & Equipment Systems

Fully Automated Feeding System

Sensor-controlled line distribution feeding trays that automatically feed birds based on age-weight curves.

Automatic Watering System

Nipple drinkers providing safe, fresh, uncontaminated water directly to birds on demand, promoting optimal hydration.

Automatic Temperature Control

Centralized TempTron electronic panel coordinates heating lamps, ventilation fans, and water pumps for uniform shed heat.

Belgium Imported Roxell Equipment

Premium grade Roxell automated lines guarantee durable operations, precise weight controls, and top production safety.

Feed Storage Silos

Industrial bulk silos block light, moisture, and pests, keeping feed fresh and preserving chemical nutrients.

Honeycomb Cooling Pads

Cellulose evaporative cooling cell wall pads filter incoming air draft and reduce temperature by 5-10°C during hot summers.

Ventilation System

High-powered exhaust fans extract stale air, gases, and humidity, maintaining continuous fresh air exchanges.

Climate-Controlled Poultry Houses

Fully enclosed multi-story concrete structures insulated to block ambient environmental temperature variations.

Biosecurity Measures

Boundary fencing, sanitizer dips, vehicles disinfectant spray, and strict entry filters for disease prevention.

Clean Water Supply

Dedicated borehole water source equipped with physical sediment filters and chemical purification systems.

Professional Farm Monitoring

Constant inspections by veterinary supervisors and experienced stock managers to track average bird growth metrics.

Roxell Belgium Premium

Providing automated feed storage systems and direct transmission lines.

Premium Machinery

Equipped with Premium Automated Technology from Roxell (Belgium)

BM Farms is equipped with premium automated poultry equipment from Roxell (Belgium), ensuring precise feeding, reliable performance, efficient farm management, and consistent poultry production.

By avoiding manual distribution, our Roxell feed transmission lines safeguard the raw nutritional value of feed, and prevent transmission of dampness or airborne spores. This directly contributes to higher bird health ratings, lower FCRs (Feed Conversion Ratios), and superior uniformity across production lots.

Safety & Biosecurity

Strict Protocols for Disease Prevention & Hygiene

Farm Sanitization

Sheds sanitized completely with broad-spectrum disinfectants before every lot.

Visitor Restrictions

Strict 'No Unauthorized Entry' policy to prevent cross-contamination.

Controlled Entry

Disinfection foot baths and hand rubs at every gate. Vehicle tires sprayed.

Regular Cleaning

Daily litter raking and sweeping of surrounding concrete aprons.

Disease Prevention

Vaccination program and veterinary checks backed by our integration partner.

Climate Monitoring

TempTron alarms alert staff if relative humidity or temperatures fluctuate.

Fresh Water Management

Drinker lines flushed regularly to clear sanitizing biofilms and sediments.

Quality Feed Handling

Dry silo preservation prevents toxic mold growth and pest contact.
